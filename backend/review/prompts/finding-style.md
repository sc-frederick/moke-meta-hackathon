## Short, actionable findings

Save the complete technical finding first. Use a short title, one observation
sentence and one concrete action for its display summary. Remove repetition,
boilerplate and background from the summary; keep facts, conditions and uncertainty
that change what the reviewer should do. Keep unresolved evidence as Needs input.
The host supplies and enforces the summary contract separately. Full technical
text, values, assumptions and evidence remain saved even if a summary is rejected.

### Style examples

These examples adapt the Sonnet rewrites of earlier Meta findings. They show
wording only. They are not evidence about this packet, verified technical
conclusions or instructions to create these findings. Inspect the current
sources and apply the evidence, citation and absence-search rules independently.
Do not copy example facts, findings or corrections into a review without support.

Drawing conflict:

- `title`: Speaker note conflicts with horn design
- `observed`: Note 14 says speaker output, but the design uses horns and strobes.
- `correction`: Change Note 14 to horn audibility and state the ambient level and
  verification method.

Count comparison that needs input:

- `title`: C1 post count cannot be compared
- `observed`: The plan shows one new C1 post, but the Column Schedule has no
  quantity column, so no count can be compared.
- `correction`: Add a quantity to the schedule, or say it is type only, and
  confirm one C1 post is intended.

Incomplete calculation, after inspecting the actual table:

- `title`: Battery and voltage drop calculation incomplete
- `observed`: The calculation covers only the new notification circuit; existing
  circuit data is blank, totals are redacted, and the limits are not compared
  with the summed load.
- `correction`: Complete the calculation for all notification circuits and show
  the installed battery against the 8.90 AH required.

Uncertain reading:

- `title`: Equipment tag is illegible
- `observed`: The equipment tag is illegible, so its schedule match cannot be
  checked.
- `correction`: Provide a legible tag and identify the matching schedule row.
