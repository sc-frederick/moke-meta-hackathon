# Fire protection coordination review

Apply the master `AGENTS.md` and `REVIEW_METHOD.md`. Own the interfaces between the site, building, fire service, alarm, sprinkler, standpipe and jurisdictional fire submittals. Fire Alarm and Fire Sprinkler reviewers own their system internals; Life Safety owns occupancy, egress and rated assemblies. Report one coordinated issue when the same defect crosses their sheets.

## Required inputs and sequence

Collect the fire authority's current criteria and comments, site/utility and building plans, life-safety analysis, alarm and sprinkler plans, fire-flow data and separate-permit information. Trace fire apparatus access and water supply to the building, then compare system assumptions and permit responsibilities. Mark an absent separately permitted package as a review boundary rather than assuming its design fails.

## Fire access and service

- Confirm the actual fire access and fire-flow requirements with the fire authority. Compare apparatus route width, grade, turning, gates, aerial access if applicable, and hydrant/FDC locations with the civil layout and building entrances.
- Distinguish hydrants, FDCs, domestic service and fire lines. Trace fire-main size, route, valves, backflow, meter if required, easements and utility connection through the riser. Compare flow-test basis and fire-flow table with site and sprinkler calculations.
- Coordinate fire pump/standpipe need, location, service power, water supply, control and separate permits with Structural, Electrical and Fire Sprinkler.

## Building and permit interfaces

- Compare occupancy, construction type, height/area, required fire systems and the stated exceptions across cover, Life Safety, alarm and sprinkler documents. Resolve a changed occupancy or use before deciding which system is required.
- Check that required rated assemblies, openings, doors, joints, dampers, firestops and protected support rooms use compatible details across architectural, structural and MEP sheets.
- Check local fire permit requirements, construction fire plan, lockbox/access, radio coverage/testing, integrated testing and responsible designers only when the authority requires them for this permit.
- Identify separately permitted or deferred fire work and the minimum design information needed in the current building permit. State the review boundary when the later package is absent.

## Revision and release

Trace each prior fire authority comment to the revised site, building or system document. Before release, reconcile fire access, hydrant/FDC/fire-main positions, occupancy and system triggers, ratings, fire-pump/standpipe interfaces and separate-permit responsibilities.
