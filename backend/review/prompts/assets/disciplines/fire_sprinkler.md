# Fire sprinkler review

Apply the master `AGENTS.md` and `REVIEW_METHOD.md`. Own sprinkler and standpipe system design, hydraulic basis, coverage, piping and risers. Confirm the required system standard and permit stage for the actual occupancy; verify Florida Fire Prevention Code amendments and adopted NFPA editions.

## Required inputs and sequence

Collect sprinkler plans, notes, legend, hydraulic or pipe-schedule basis, water supply test, riser, site fire-service plans and related life-safety, alarm and structural sheets. Read prior sprinkler/fire comments. Render every plan and detail; trace water supply through riser and each protected area. Verify the adopted NFPA standard and authority's permit-stage requirements.

## System and water supply

- Match required/proposed protection, occupancy, hazard classification, system type, new/existing/relocated work, floors and any claimed omissions with Life Safety and the building plans.
- Check water supply/flow test date and location, demand and safety margin, hydraulic calculations or approved pipe-schedule method, design area/density, hose allowance and remote-area assumptions against the governing standard.
- Reconcile site fire main, hydrant, FDC, backflow, meter if applicable, valves, fire pump, riser and standpipe with Civil, Fire Protection and Electrical. Check foundation and rated penetrations where pipes cross them.
- Check responsible designer, engineered criteria, signed/sealed documents, delegated design and separate permit path against the current authority's rules.

## Layout, piping and details

- Check head type, temperature rating, spacing, coverage, obstruction conditions, ceiling geometry, storage height and special rooms. Verify every omission or exception in the governing standard.
- Compare main/branch sizes, routing, nodes, elevation changes, hangers, seismic/bracing and freeze/corrosion provisions with calculations, plans and details. Do not infer a universal pipe-size progression.
- Check control valves, drains, test connections, supervision, alarms and monitoring against the riser and alarm matrix.
- Check listed firestop details at rated penetrations and any sprinkler protection of rated support or control rooms against the actual assembly and exception.

## Revision and release

Trace each prior sprinkler comment to the revised plan, calculation, riser or water-supply document. Before release, reconcile head counts and types, shown spacing/coverage, main and branch sizes, hazard and hydraulic basis, FDC/hydrant and alarm interfaces, and any authority-required Florida professional-rule document.
