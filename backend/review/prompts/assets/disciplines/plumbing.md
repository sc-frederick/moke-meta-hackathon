# Plumbing review

Apply the master `AGENTS.md` and `REVIEW_METHOD.md`. Own building water, sanitary, vent, gas and roof drainage. Civil owns external utility alignment; compare the shared connection points, demand and elevations.

## Required inputs and sequence

Collect current plumbing plans, notes, fixture and equipment schedules, water/DWV/gas risers, sizing calculations, utility criteria and related architecture, civil and mechanical sheets. Read prior plumbing comments. Render every sheet and detail, trace each system from source to termination, then compare plan marks, quantities and pipe sizes with schedules and isometrics.

## Basis and schedules

- Match existing, new, relocated and removed fixtures and equipment with floor plans, fixture counts, room uses, schedule marks, risers and permit scope. Recalculate fixture requirements using actual occupant loads and approved exceptions.
- Check service and pipe materials, fixture supply/waste sizes, water-heater type/capacity, temperature control, insulation and gas appliance schedule against the adopted codes and selected equipment.
- Verify required plans, risers/isometrics, calculations and details for the systems actually in scope. Do not infer that a well has or lacks a meter without checking the serving authority.

## Water, sanitary and vent

- Trace the water source through meter where applicable, backflow, shutoffs, heater, distribution and each fixture. Compare demand, pressure, pipe sizing and hot-water delivery method with the calculations and serving utility information.
- Trace waste and vents from each fixture to the building drain and approved connection. Check fixture trap sizes, trap-seal protection, cleanout locations and details, changes in direction, wet venting, vent termination, slope, access and riser/plan agreement against the actual code provision. Avoid blanket direction-change and pipe-size rules.
- Check fixture clearances, accessible lavatory temperature protection, laundry discharge, floor drains, water-heater pan/relief discharge and thermal-expansion control where the corresponding equipment or valve arrangement exists.
- For an alteration, identify existing tie-in location, size and capacity; check trench/slab restoration and listed firestops at rated penetrations.

## Roof drainage, gas and site tie-ins

- For roof drainage, compare roof area and vertical-wall contribution with primary and emergency drain sizing, locations, leaders and lawful discharge. Match the roof plan, calculations and plumbing riser.
- For gas work, check appliance loads, developed length, pressure, regulator, pipe sizing, shutoffs and ventilation/exhaust interface against the actual fuel system.
- Match water/sewer/storm connections, backflow and meter positions, pump/force-main data, pretreatment, easement/ROW notes, and flood-exposed equipment elevations with Civil, Mechanical and the utility application.

## Revision and release

Trace each prior plumbing comment to the revised plan, fixture schedule, riser, calculation or detail. Before release, reconcile fixture marks and quantities, water-heater type and location, pipe sizes, cleanouts, gas schedule if applicable, and building/site connection data.
