# Civil review

Apply the master `AGENTS.md` and `REVIEW_METHOD.md`. Own exterior site geometry, zoning data, grading, drainage, stormwater, floodplain impacts, external utilities, right-of-way, access, and site agency coordination. Use the actual city/county land development code, approved development conditions, and current agency manuals. Mark a missing survey, report, or approval `Needs input` when it prevents a conclusion.

## Required inputs and sequence

Collect the current civil set and index, survey/legal description, easements, zoning and development conditions, agency correspondence, geotechnical and stormwater reports, drainage calculations, utility data and permit forms. Obtain landscape, architecture, structural, MEP, fire and lighting sheets that meet the site. List missing inputs and the checks they prevent.

Inventory the documents and past review cycles. Build a list of applicable jurisdiction and agency requirements; compare calculations, plans and forms against the same source values. Recheck each changed area and every prior comment on a resubmittal. Use repeatable arithmetic for areas, slopes, elevations, flows, storage and parking counts; show inputs and units.

## Site and zoning

- Match address, parcel/folio, legal description, boundary, bearings, north arrow, scale, datum, limits of work, easements, rights-of-way, adjacent uses, flood zones, wetlands, and regulated buffers to the survey and permit forms.
- Recalculate lot area, building coverage, floor area ratio, open space, impervious/pervious area, and landscape area from shown geometry. Compare existing, proposed, and phase totals with the jurisdiction's definitions, including its treatment of permeable pavement.
- Check zoning/use, setbacks from the correct line, height and overlay limits, buffers, parking/loading/bicycle counts, and conditions of approval. Recount parking after islands, cross access, wheel stops, dumpsters, and future phases are included.
- Reconcile finished-floor elevations, entrance thresholds, building footprint, height and area with architectural and structural sheets.

## Access and accessibility

- Dimension stalls, aisles, drives, sidewalks, ramps, landings, loading, and dumpster service. Trace a continuous accessible route from required arrival points to each accessible entrance; calculate running and cross slopes from the actual spot grades.
- Check accessible striping, signs, curb ramps, detectable warnings, door approach, wheel-stop/vehicle overhang, and sidewalk clear width against the governing accessibility criteria.
- Check dumpster screening, gate swing and collection access against parking and bicycle spaces. Check access permits, right-of-way work, sight distance, driveway radii, road reservations, pedestrian crossings, pavement sections, and turning paths for fire, solid waste, delivery, and utility vehicles. Compare any required traffic study's trip generation, distribution, access movements and queue assumptions with the plan and approved conditions.

## Grading, drainage, stormwater, and floodplain

- Trace every drainage area to collection, treatment, overflow, and a lawful outfall. Compare drainage boundaries, land cover, rainfall, groundwater, curve numbers/runoff coefficients, and discharge assumptions across plans, calculations, and permit forms.
- Recalculate applicable storage, water quality, peak flow, drawdown/recovery, infiltration, and floodplain compensating volume with the required agency method. Show inputs, units, datum, result, and limit. Verify geotechnical and seasonal high-water assumptions where used.
- Check spot elevations, slopes, flow arrows, swales, ponds, property-line grading, inlet/pipe inverts, pipe sizes, profiles, cross sections, erosion controls, and maintenance access. Identify offsite impacts and missing receiving-system information.
- Reconcile mapped flood zone, base/design flood elevation, finished-floor elevation, floodplain impacts, and floodproofing assumptions with building and structural documents.

## Utilities and site interfaces

- Locate existing and proposed water, sewer, storm, fire, gas, power and communications service, owners, connection points, easements, crossings, and abandoned segments. Confirm the irrigation water source where irrigation is proposed. Compare pipe size/material/cover/separation, invert/slope, and utility details with the actual serving agency's criteria.
- Match water demand, sewer flow, meter/backflow, well or septic status, hydrant/fire-main route, and building service points with plumbing, fire, electrical, and utility applications. Confirm well metering rules from the serving authority; do not assume one rule for every well. Check the authority's subsurface-utility investigation level at crossings and connections.
- For lift stations and force mains, compare projected flow, total dynamic head, pump/system curves, operating point, velocity, wet-well and float levels, controls, emergency operation, and buoyancy calculations.
- Compare utility certification/application forms with plans and calculations, including existing versus proposed service segments and required availability letters.
- Check tree inventory, removal/replacement and mitigation calculations, buffers, screening, wetland/conservation impacts, site lighting/photometric boundaries, pole locations, fire access and hydrant coordination, and construction conflicts with underground utilities.

## Revision and output

- Match each prior civil or agency comment to the actual revised plan, calculation, form, or permit condition. Flag repeated requests and mismatched response claims.
- For each numeric finding, include the measured drawing inputs, governing definition/method, units, calculation, result, and cited limit. For missing information, name the exact check that cannot be completed.
- Before release, reconcile the site plan, grading plan, utility plan, landscape plan, survey, reports and permit forms. Check required signatures, seals, approvals and certifications against the current permit process.
