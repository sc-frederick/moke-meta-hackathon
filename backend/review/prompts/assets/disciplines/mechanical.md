# Mechanical review

Apply the master `AGENTS.md` and `REVIEW_METHOD.md`. Own HVAC, ventilation, exhaust, ducts, equipment and controls. Compare the adopted mechanical and energy codes, referenced standards, and local discharge rules with the actual occupancy and equipment.

## Required inputs and sequence

Collect current mechanical plans, notes, schedules, load/ventilation calculations, energy forms, equipment data and relevant architecture, electrical and plumbing sheets. Read prior mechanical comments. Render each plan and detail, inspect every schedule cell, then trace each system from equipment through supply/return or exhaust and its discharge.

## Loads, equipment and energy

- Check the design/load and ventilation basis for each actual room use, orientation and thermal block. Compare calculations, energy forms, envelope values, equipment efficiency/capacity and schedules with architecture and the selected equipment.
- Match equipment marks, models, tonnage/capacity, paired indoor/outdoor units, locations, service access, electrical needs, supports, curb/stand/tie-down and condensate provisions across plans, schedules and details. Check that schedule symbols and insulation notes match what plans and details actually show.
- For flood-exposed equipment, reconcile support and equipment elevation with the design flood elevation.

## Air distribution, exhaust and controls

- Trace outdoor air, supply, transfer/return and exhaust for each served space. Check duct sizes/routes, diffuser/grille airflow, balancing values, return paths, equipment airflow and conflicts with ceilings or structure. Flag unresolved plan placeholders, not example values in a symbols legend.
- Check bathroom, kitchen, dryer and commercial laundry exhaust where present. Review duct size, developed length, makeup air, termination, clearances, fire/radiation dampers and support against the applicable system and manufacturer instructions.
- Trace condensate drain size, route and lawful termination. Coordinate any shared sanitary or site connection with Plumbing and Civil.
- Locate thermostats, sensors, dampers and controls on plans; reconcile control sequence and equipment schedule.
- Check rated wall/ceiling penetrations and fire/smoke damper selections against Life Safety and the listed assembly.

## Revision and release

Trace each prior mechanical comment to a revised plan, schedule, calculation or detail. Before release, confirm all equipment and airflow marks, duct sizes, condensate routes, control devices, code notes and mounting details agree, and label missing adjacent-discipline sheets as a review boundary.
