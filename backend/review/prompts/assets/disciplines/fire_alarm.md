# Fire alarm review

Apply the master `AGENTS.md` and `REVIEW_METHOD.md`. Own detection, notification, alarm control, monitoring and sequence of operations. Establish whether a system is required, existing, altered or separately permitted from the actual occupancy, code path and authority requirements.

## Required inputs and sequence

Collect alarm plans, device legend and data, riser, input/output matrix, battery and notification-circuit calculations, building data and related life-safety, sprinkler and electrical sheets. Read prior alarm/fire comments. Render each alarm sheet; trace every device through its circuit and intended sequence. Verify the adopted NFPA 72, Florida Fire Prevention Code and local requirements used by the design.

## System basis and documentation

- Match building data, occupancy, sprinkler status, zones/floors and fire alarm scope with Life Safety and Fire Sprinkler. Identify each device and conductor as existing, new, relocated or removed.
- Check permit package, designer responsibility, signed/sealed documents, product data and any Florida professional-rule form only when the current authority and project scope require them.
- Check legend, device quantities, riser/line diagram, control panel location and environmental limits, input/output matrix and sequence for sprinkler monitoring, notification, elevator/HVAC/door release and other shown interfaces.
- Check battery, secondary-power/SLA and notification-appliance circuit calculations against device loads, wire lengths and selected equipment. Compare panel capacity and circuit assignments with the floor plans.

## Devices and coordination

- Check initiating device type, location, spacing and coverage for ceiling height, room and air movement conditions. Check pull stations, waterflow/tamper devices, smoke/heat/CO detection and any occupancy-specific voice or emergency communication requirement.
- Check horn/strobe and other notification locations, coverage, synchronization, accessibility and circuit grouping against actual spaces and current design criteria.
- Coordinate smoke/CO dwelling alarms, exit paths, sprinkler monitoring, control room and delayed-egress logic with Life Safety, Electrical and Fire Sprinkler. Flag contradictions with both locations.

## Revision and release

Trace each prior alarm comment to the revised sheet, matrix, calculation or data sheet. Before release, reconcile building data, symbol/device counts, panel and circuit totals, input/output matrix, detector spacing, notification coverage, and any authority-required Florida professional-rule document.
