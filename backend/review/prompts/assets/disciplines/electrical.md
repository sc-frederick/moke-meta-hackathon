# Electrical review

Apply the master `AGENTS.md` and `REVIEW_METHOD.md`. Own service, distribution, power, lighting, grounding, circuit protection and electrical notes. Verify the adopted electrical code edition and local utility requirements; do not treat an advisory voltage-drop target as a mandatory code limit.

## Required inputs and sequence

Collect current power and lighting plans, one-line/riser, panel schedules, load and voltage-drop calculations, utility data and related architecture, fire, civil and equipment sheets. Read prior electrical comments. Render every assigned sheet; trace service to final circuits and compare loads, ratings and device callouts across plans, schedules and calculations.

## Service and calculations

- Reconcile existing, new and removed service equipment, meter/transformer, disconnects, panel locations, one-line/riser, transfer switch, utility point of service and site route. Compare service and feeder ratings with calculated existing plus new loads.
- Check load method against occupancy and actual area. Match panel schedules, circuit lists, breaker sizes, conductor/conduit sizes, grounding/bonding and overcurrent protection across plans, schedules and riser.
- Check available fault-current basis, interrupting/short-circuit ratings, required markings and equipment working clearances. Identify missing utility data as `Needs input` when the equipment rating cannot be judged.
- Review voltage-drop calculations where required by the governing source or project criteria. Distinguish a mandatory limit from a recommended target in any finding.
- For fire pumps, alarm panels and other life-safety loads, coordinate dedicated supply, disconnect/transfer arrangement and equipment ratings with Fire Alarm, Fire Sprinkler and Life Safety.

## Power and lighting

- Check each outlet, device and piece of equipment for location, panel/circuit designation, load, disconnect and required GFCI/AFCI or other protection. Compare appliance and HVAC requirements with their schedules.
- Check lighting fixture marks, switch locations and circuit callouts, wiring or control connections using the drawing's legend, emergency lighting and source, exit signs, egress route, site lighting and any sign/display outlets where applicable. Match controls and power to the actual room use.
- Coordinate smoke/CO dwelling alarms, fire alarm interfaces and rated penetrations with the relevant discipline.
- For flood-exposed sites, compare meter, panel and other electrical equipment elevations with the design flood elevation. For exterior poles, coordinate locations, height/support and photometric limits with Civil.

## Revision and release

Trace each prior electrical comment to the revised plan, riser, schedule or calculation. Before release, confirm each symbol has a plan location and circuit, each new circuit resolves to a panel, all panel/riser/load values agree, and every protection or smoke/CO callout uses the project's actual code path.
