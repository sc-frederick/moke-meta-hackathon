# Structural review

Apply the master `AGENTS.md` and `REVIEW_METHOD.md`. Own the structural design basis, foundations, framing, lateral system, connections and structural product approvals. Do not assert inadequate capacity without a calculation, specified load, or clear code basis.

## Required inputs and sequence

Collect current structural drawings, calculations, geotechnical report, product approvals, delegated packages and relevant architecture/civil plans. Read prior structural comments and identify altered or existing structure. Render every plan, schedule, section and connection detail; follow loads to the foundation and compare cited values with calculations and adjacent disciplines.

## Design criteria and existing work

- Check risk category, occupancy-related loads, wind speed/exposure/enclosure, roof and wall pressure zones, gravity, rain, flood and seismic criteria as applicable. Match adopted code and referenced standard editions across sheets and calculations.
- Compare survey/geotechnical soil bearing, site flood/design elevations, building dimensions and roof slopes with civil and architecture. For alterations, identify existing structure and new openings, loads and connections; confirm how existing capacity was evaluated.
- Check whether truss, precast, metal-building, equipment-stand or other delegated work has design criteria, reactions, interfaces, responsible designer, and a permitted submission path. Match supplied shop drawings to the permit set.

## Foundations and framing

- Trace footing, slab, pier, pile and grade-beam marks to schedules and details. Check sizes, elevations, materials, reinforcement, cover, anchors, and connections to existing work.
- Trace each floor and roof load path through members, diaphragms, shear/braced walls, collectors, hold-downs, columns and foundations. Check member sizes, spans, bearing, bracing, sheathing and fastening against plans, schedules and calculations.
- For stairs, balconies, decks, canopies, rooftop equipment and altered openings, check live/uplift loads, support, attachment and lateral transfer. Compare required and stated capacities when both are available.

## Connections and special conditions

- Inspect every connection detail for location, base plate, bolt/weld/fastener size, embedment, edge distance, material and legible dimensions. Confirm product approvals and installation details where specified.
- For flood-exposed work, compare flood load, breakaway construction, flood openings, materials, and finished-floor elevations with civil and architecture. Verify the governing flood criteria before requiring an elevation or certificate.
- Check structural requirements at rated assemblies and MEP penetrations for coordination with the selected wall or floor system. Cite the exact conflicting sheets or details.

## Revision and release

Reconcile prior comments with actual revised sheets, calculations or delegated packages. Check notes and specifications for contradictory values, code editions, spelling or text that changes the design meaning. Before release, confirm each member mark and connection reference resolves and every delegated interface has enough criteria for the current permit stage.
