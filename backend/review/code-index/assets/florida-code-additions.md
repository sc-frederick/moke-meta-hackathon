# Florida code reference additions

Purpose: additions to `FBC_2023_plan_review_reference.md` in the same format. Entries cover Florida law that the plan review reference does not: statutes that govern permits, threshold buildings, product approval and professional seals; administrative rules for engineering documents; the Florida Fire Prevention Code adoption; and the 9th Edition (2026) volumes. Like the reference, it contains no copied code text.

- Statute links resolve to the official text on the Florida Legislature's site, which `read_source` can retrieve. Titles and the facts in each check were confirmed against that text on 2026-10-30 (2026 Florida Statutes).
- Florida Administrative Code links resolve to the rule's page on flrules.org. The rule text itself is published as a Word document, so open the link to read it. Rule titles were confirmed on 2026-10-30.
- 9th Edition (2026) volume and 2024 supplement links come from the ICC Digital Codes Florida page, read on 2026-10-30. The 9th Edition takes effect December 31, 2026; section links in the plan review reference remain 8th Edition (2023).

## Florida Statutes - 2026 Florida Statutes

Base: [Chapter 553, Building Construction Standards](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/0553.html)

### Code adoption and interpretation

- **553.73 Florida Building Code** - Confirm the governing code and any local technical amendments; a local amendment is not effective until its appeal period ends or the commission rules on an appeal. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/Sections/0553.73.html)
- **553.775 Interpretations** - Check whether a Florida Building Commission declaratory statement or interpretation governs the disputed provision. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/Sections/0553.775.html)

### Permits, threshold buildings and plan review

- **553.71 Definitions** - Decide whether the project is a threshold building: more than three stories or 50 feet in height, or an assembly occupancy over 5,000 square feet with an occupant content over 500. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/Sections/0553.71.html)
- **553.79 Permits; applications; issuance; inspections** - For a threshold building, confirm the engineer or architect of record prepared a structural inspection plan, which the enforcing agency must approve before it issues the building permit. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/Sections/0553.79.html)
- **553.791 Alternative plans review and inspection** - Where a private provider performs plan review, confirm the required plan review affidavit accompanies the permit documents. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/Sections/0553.791.html)
- **553.792 Building permit application to local government** - Use for the local government's review timeframes after it receives a complete and sufficient permit application. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/Sections/0553.792.html)

### Products and windstorm mitigation

- **553.842 Product evaluation and approval** - Confirm products subject to approval cite a valid statewide or local product approval for the application shown. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/Sections/0553.842.html)
- **553.844 Windstorm loss mitigation; requirements for roofs and opening protection** - Check reroofing and opening protection work against the statutory windstorm mitigation requirements. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/Sections/0553.844.html)

### Professional seals

- **471.025 Seals** - Confirm engineering documents carry the licensee's seal in a board-approved form, applied as the statute and board rules require. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0471/Sections/0471.025.html)
- **481.221 Seals; display of certificate number** - Confirm architectural documents carry the registered architect's approved seal and certificate number. [Link](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0481/Sections/0481.221.html)

## Florida Administrative Code - Current rules

Base: [Florida Administrative Code](https://flrules.org/)

### Engineer seals and signatures (Chapter 61G15-23)

- **61G15-23.001 Signature, Date and Seal Shall Be Affixed** - Confirm each sealed engineering document is signed, dated and sealed as the rule requires. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-23.001)
- **61G15-23.002 Seals Acceptable to the Board** - Check the seal's form against the board's accepted seal requirements. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-23.002)
- **61G15-23.003 Procedures for Physically Signing and Sealing Plans, Specifications, Reports or Other Documents** - For wet-signed sets, check the physical signing and sealing procedure. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-23.003)
- **61G15-23.004 Procedures for Digitally Signing and Sealing Electronically Transmitted Plans, Specifications, Reports or Other Documents** - For digitally signed electronic sets, check the digital signature and sealing procedure. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-23.004)
- **61G15-23.005 Procedures for Electronically Signing and Sealing Electronically Transmitted Plans, Specifications, Reports or Other Documents** - For electronically signed sets, check the electronic signature and sealing procedure. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-23.005)

### Engineering documents and delegation (Chapter 61G15-30)

- **61G15-30.003 Minimum Requirements for Engineering Documents** - Check that the engineering documents contain the minimum content the board requires. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-30.003)
- **61G15-30.005 Delegation of Engineering Documents: Obligations of the Engineer of Record** - Where design is delegated, check the engineer of record's obligations for the delegated documents. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-30.005)
- **61G15-30.006 Delegation of Engineering Documents: Obligations of the Delegated Engineer of Record** - Check the delegated engineer's documents meet their stated obligations. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-30.006)
- **61G15-30.007 Prime Professional's Responsibility** - Identify the prime professional coordinating the engineering documents. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-30.007)

### Structural responsibility rules (Chapter 61G15-31)

- **61G15-31.003 Design of Structures Utilizing Prefabricated Wood Trusses** - For wood truss structures, check the division of responsibility between the structural engineer of record and the truss design engineer. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-31.003)
- **61G15-31.008 Design of Foundations** - Check the foundation design documents against the board's responsibility rule for foundations. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G15-31.008)

### Building code and product approval (Chapters 61G20-1 and 61G20-3)

- **61G20-1.001 Florida Building Code Adopted** - Confirm the code edition and supplements adopted by rule for the permit's application date. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G20-1.001)
- **61G20-3.001 Scope (Product Approval)** - Use with F.S. 553.842 to decide which products need approval. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=61G20-3.001)

### Florida Fire Prevention Code (Chapter 69A-60)

- **69A-60.003 Standards of the National Fire Protection Association, NFPA 1, the Fire Code, Florida 2021 Edition, Adopted** - Confirm fire code requirements cite NFPA 1 as adopted for Florida. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=69A-60.003)
- **69A-60.004 Standards of the National Fire Protection Association, NFPA 101, the Life Safety Code, Florida 2021 Edition, Adopted** - Confirm life safety requirements cite NFPA 101 as adopted for Florida. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=69A-60.004)
- **69A-60.0081 Notice Required for Structures With Light-frame Truss-type Construction** - Where light-frame truss-type construction is used, check the required notice is addressed. [Link](https://flrules.org/gateway/ruleNo.asp?id=69A-60.0081)
